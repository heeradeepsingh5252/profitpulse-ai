# Demo Expense Approval Policy

This document is synthetic training content for the ProfitPulse AI demonstration.
It is not a real company's policy and must not be treated as legal or accounting advice.

## Purchase approvals

A department manager may approve an unbudgeted operating purchase up to INR 25,000.
Purchases above INR 25,000 and up to INR 100,000 require approval from the finance lead.
Purchases above INR 100,000 require approval from both the finance lead and the managing director.

## Supporting evidence

Every reimbursement request must include an invoice or receipt, the business purpose,
the transaction date, and the approving manager. Missing evidence should be returned
to the requester rather than treated as an approved expense.

## Vendor and logistics costs

New recurring vendor commitments must be reviewed by finance before signing. Logistics
invoices should include shipment reference, delivery date, and the relevant purchase order.
